CPI vs PPI: Lagged Regression Modeling

Project: Statistical correlation between CPI and PPI and regression modeling (Aug 2025 – Mar 2026)

Python pandas scikit-learn matplotlib FRED economic data

What I did

  • Analyzed U.S. CPI and PPI data (1947–2025) to test lagged inflation dynamics.
  • Converted CPI and PPI into YoY percentage changes for time-series analysis.
  • Built lagged regression models (3–12 months) using Python.
  • Applied Ridge Regression with cross-validation to address multicollinearity.
  • Achieved R² ≈ 0.97–0.98, explaining ~97% of CPI variance.
  • Evaluated performance across COVID-19, the 2008 crisis, and oil shocks.
  • Visualized results using Actual vs. Predicted and time-series plots.

Status: Paper submitted to the National Journal of High School Science Research (under review).
